## Jeffrey H. Tigay, *The JPS Torah Commentary: Deuteronomy* (1996)
File: `tigay-deuteronomy.txt`

A verse-by-verse commentary in the JPS series: introduction, commentary on Deut 1:1–34:12, and 33 excursuses with their notes. Judicious and philological, drawing on rabbinic and medieval Jewish interpretation and on ancient Near Eastern treaty and law. Critical but not driven by source theory; it dates the core of the book to the late monarchy. The primary source for the book's structure, the treaty background, how Deut 1–3 differ from Exodus and Numbers, the Decalogue differences, and Deut's laws beside Exodus and Leviticus (Excursus 16 is the model). Its dating judgments belong in a footnote, per the plan.

**Using the file:** it is OCR of a scan. `[p. N]` markers are printed pages (cite them), and pp. 414–415 are missing from the scan. The scan has no bibliography or indexes. Hebrew in the verse columns did not OCR: `[heb]` and `[heb?]` mark it, so take Hebrew from the corpus, never from this file. `[figure …]` / `[table …]` mark the maps and tables; `research/figures-tigay.md` says what each shows and lists the excursuses.
