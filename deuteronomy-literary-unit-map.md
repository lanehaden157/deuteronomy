# Deuteronomy — Literary Unit Map

> **DRAFT: unchecked against the commentaries.** Written without Tigay, McConville, Block or Weinfeld. Units, tiers, peaks and reasoning are proposals until Lane's project chat checks them. No Digests section yet; the treaty overlay is wave 2.

Grouped by Moses' speeches, using the book's own superscriptions (1:1; 4:44; Eng 29:1 = Heb 28:69; 33:1) and the Masoretic breaks (`candidate-boundaries.md`, Hebrew numbering there).

**Versification.** English numbering; `[Heb ...]` where it differs (Eng 12:32–13:18 = Heb 13:1–19, Eng 22:30–23:25 = Heb 23:1–26, Eng 29:1–29 = Heb 28:69–29:28).

**Markers.** A marker is cited by the verse it follows. Where none falls on a boundary (units 1/2, 3, 12) narrative logic decides.

**Speeches are not chapter-tidy.** The first speech (1:1–4:40) is followed by a hinge paragraph (4:41–49) with refuge cities and the new heading; unit 06 is grouped with the second speech. The Ebal ceremony (ch. 27) is told partly in the third person; it is grouped with the second speech.

**Peaks** (the path the site opens on): 07 Ten Words, 08 Shema, 12 what Yahweh asks, 28 firstfruits declaration, 30 blessings and curses, 32 choose life, 34 the Song, 36 Moses' death. Every peak is full.

## Reasoning (draft, one line per unit)

- 01: No Masoretic break in ch. 1; superscription (1:1–5) plus the first retold event (Exod 18 / Num 11). Retelling, so key verses only.
- 02: Retelling of Num 13–14, a Side by side candidate. Split from 01 on narrative logic (no marker at 1:19); 2:1 setumah closes it.
- 03: Itinerary and victories; 2:1 setumah opens, no break at 3:11. A summary serves; the theology of borders fits one line.
- 04: Allotment, Joshua named, Moses' plea refused; 3:29 petuhah closes. Moses' exclusion matters for ch. 34.
- 05: Hinge sermon on the Horeb event and idolatry; the book's first big argument (4:24 and 4:40 breaks). Not a peak.
- 06: Masoretic paragraph (4:40 and 4:49 petuhot) holding the superscription of the second speech (4:44). Short.
- 07: Covenant at Horeb restated; compare Exod 20. Peak. Side by side candidate.
- 08: The book's centre of gravity: 6:4–9 and the children's question 6:20–25. Peak.
- 09: Chosen not for size (7:6–8) and the herem command; key verses plus tables.
- 10: 8:3 is quoted in Matt 4; remember/forget theme. Not a peak.
- 11: Retelling of Exod 32–34, Side by side candidate; 9:29 and 10:11 petuhot.
- 12: Fear-love-walk summary, 10:16–22, and 11:26–32 'see, I set before you'. Peak. No marker at 11:32 or 12:1; narrative logic.
- 13: Centralization; closes at the petuhah that Hebrew numbering places at 13:1.
- 14: Three cases, loyalty inside the law; setumah at the end (Heb 13:19).
- 15: Parallel to Lev 11 (differences only); 14:21 petuhah.
- 16: 'No poor among you' (15:4) against 15:11; Exod 21 slave-law parallel. 15:18 petuhah.
- 17: Calendar table (wave 2) beside Lev 23 and Num 28–29; 16:17 setumah.
- 18: One justice-and-courts block (16:18–20; 17:2–13); ends at the 17:13 setumah.
- 19: Short and loaded, ties to 1 Sam 8 and Solomon; its own setumah-bounded paragraph.
- 20: Offices; 18:15–22 matters for Acts 3 and 7 and for 34:10.
- 21: Cities of refuge (Num 35 parallel) and the two-witness rule; 19:21 setumah.
- 22: Casuistic rules for war; 20:20 setumah. A summary serves unless Lane wants it fuller.
- 23: Four cases; 21:22–23 is quoted at Gal 3:13.
- 24: Mixed bag (kind acts, mixed fabrics, tassels, sexual cases); ends at the 22:30 (Heb 23:1) setumah.
- 25: Who may enter the assembly, camp cleanliness, vows and gleaning; ends at Heb 23:26.
- 26: 24:1–4 is the divorce text; 24:17–22 the stranger–orphan–widow cluster; 24:22 setumah.
- 27: Short laws; 25:17–19 Amalek links to Exod 17 and 1 Sam 15, flagged for a stretch if wanted.
- 28: 'A wandering Aramean': the credo and the mutual declaration (26:17–19). Peak; closes the statutes at the 26:19 setumah.
- 29: Writing the Torah on stones; compare Josh 8:30–35. 27:26 setumah.
- 30: The conditional blessing and curse; wave 2 mirror with Lev 26. Peak. The 28:68 (Heb) setumah closes.
- 31: Superscription Eng 29:1 / Heb 28:69; 'hidden things' 29:29 (Heb 29:28).
- 32: 'Choose life' 30:19 and the nearness of the word 30:11–14 (Rom 10). Peak.
- 33: Handover, writing and reading the Torah; 31:30 setumah. The Song's introduction falls in it.
- 34: Poem, the covenant lawsuit. Peak. 32:43 and 32:47 breaks.
- 35: 32:48–52 is its own petuhah-bounded paragraph; grouped with the Blessing as Moses' last words. Gen 49 beside ch. 33 (wave 2).
- 36: Nebo, burial, 'no prophet since like Moses'. Peak.
